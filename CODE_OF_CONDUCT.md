# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this to be a place where a student can ask a basic question without
being made to feel small, and where a professional can disagree about a proof
without it becoming personal. Be respectful, precise, and patient. Assume good
faith; say what you mean; cite the artifact when you disagree about a number.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, threats, insults, or derogatory comments about any person or
  protected characteristic.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Sexualised language or imagery in any project space.
* Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberate misrepresentation of what
  a test or certificate shows.
* Using project spaces to advertise unrelated commercial services. Licensing
  enquiries go to `research@twistpool.org`, not into issue threads.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any other
space associated with the repository, and to individuals representing the
project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **research@twistpool.org**. Reports
are handled confidentially. Depending on severity, the response may be a
private warning, an edited or removed comment, a temporary ban, or a permanent
ban from project spaces. We will give a reason where we can, and we will not
retaliate against a reporter.

This document is modelled on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
